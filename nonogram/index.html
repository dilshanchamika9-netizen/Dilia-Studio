How to play

  • The numbers beside a row or column tell the lengths of the runs of filled squares, in order.
  • Tap or drag to fill squares. Switch to the X tool to mark squares you know are empty (key: X).
  • A clue fades when its line matches.
  • Every puzzle can be solved with logic alone.

About Nonogram

Nonograms, also called Picross, hide a small picture. Each clue number is a run of filled squares in that row or column, with at least one gap between runs. Every puzzle here is generated and checked so a solver using only line logic can finish it, so you never need to guess.
