How to play

  • Press Roll dice. Tap one of your checkers, then tap where it goes.
  • You are pink and move from the top right, round the board, to the bottom right.
  • Hit a lone blue checker to send it to the bar. A checker on the bar must come in first.
  • When all 15 of your checkers are in your home board (bottom right), bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.

About Backgammon

Standard backgammon without the doubling cube. Gammons and backgammons are named but score the same as a single win. The computer uses the same dice rules as you. Two-player mode shares one screen.
