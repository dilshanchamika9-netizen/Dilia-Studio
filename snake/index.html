How to play

  • Arrow keys or W A S D to steer. On a phone: swipe, or use the buttons.
  • Eat the pink apples to grow. Don't touch your own tail.
  • Classic: the walls are deadly. Modern: the edges wrap round, and a gold star (+5) shows up after every 5th apple.
  • Space or P pauses.

About snake

Steer the snake to the apples. Each apple makes it longer and the game a little faster. In Classic mode the walls end the game; in Modern mode the edges wrap round, so you can leave one side and come back on the other, and a gold star worth 5 points appears after every fifth apple for a few seconds.

Fill the whole board and you win. Your best score for each mode stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
