How it works

Leave counts only on working days. A weekend or public holiday inside a leave period costs no leave day and never causes a shortage. Weekends and holidays follow the country you pick and apply to the whole team. Days that follow the moon, such as Eid, can shift by a day, so check the official announcement.

A shortage is a work day when fewer people are in than your minimum. Everything is saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, so use Download CSV to keep a copy or share it.
