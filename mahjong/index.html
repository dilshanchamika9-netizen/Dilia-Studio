How to play

  • Tap two free tiles with the same face to take them away.
  • A tile is free when nothing lies on it and its left or right side is open.
  • Any flower matches any flower, and any season matches any season.
  • Stuck? Shuffle mixes the tiles that are left. Hint shows an open pair.

About Mahjong

Clear all 144 tiles of the classic turtle layout. Every deal is built backwards from an empty board, so a way to clear it always exists. Tiles are shown with numbers and symbols: ● dots, ▮ bamboo, ❖ characters, E S W N winds, ◆ ◇ dragons, ✿ flowers and ❄ seasons. The tiles are small on a phone held upright; a tablet, a computer or a phone turned sideways is more comfortable.
