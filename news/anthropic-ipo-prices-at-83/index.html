Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, above the marketed range

The Claude maker sold 980 million shares in a deal worth about $81 billion. The stock trades on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

Dilia Pulse deskWednesday 18 November 20262 min read

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI models, priced its initial public offering at $83 a share on 18 November. The price was above the $70 to $78 range the company had marketed to investors.

The offering covers 980 million Class A shares. Anthropic itself sold 956,351,354 of them; the remaining 23,648,646 were sold by existing stockholders, and Anthropic receives nothing from those. At $83 a share, the whole offering is worth about $81.3 billion before fees.

Listing

The shares began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on 19 November under the symbol ANT, and the deal was set to close on 20 November. The underwriters have 30 days to buy up to 147 million additional shares, which would make the offering larger still.

Why it matters

Anthropic is one of a small group of companies building the most capable AI models. Until now, investors could only back such labs through private funding rounds or through the large technology companies that partner with them. A listed share gives ordinary investors direct exposure, and it also means Anthropic will publish quarterly results like any other public company.

The listing came during a difficult week for technology shares, which recovered sharply on the following Monday.
