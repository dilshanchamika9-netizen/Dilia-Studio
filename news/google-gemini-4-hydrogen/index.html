Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast model for everyday coding and agents

Hydrogen joins the Carbon and Argon frontier models and brings Gemini 4 to every user, with four thinking levels to trade speed against depth.

Dilia Pulse deskThursday 12 November 20261 min read

Google has introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a new model built for speed and efficiency in everyday coding and agent tasks. It sits alongside Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon, the frontier models Google launched in October for long, deep reasoning on the hardest problems.

With Hydrogen, Google says Gemini 4 is now available to everyone, across all plans in Google AI Studio, the Gemini API and the consumer Gemini app.

Four speeds

The model offers four thinking levels: low, medium, high, which is the default, and a new xhigh setting. The idea is that developers pay for and wait only as long as a task needs, with performance scaling in a predictable way as the level rises.

Where it shows up

Google highlighted spreadsheet work as one use: in Google Sheets, Hydrogen can turn financial data into interactive models that change with different forecast scenarios, and Google says it produces output more than 30 per cent faster than earlier models there. For consumers, the company says the model follows instructions more closely over long conversations.

Google also says Hydrogen brings its latest Gemini 4 safety work, combining training with real-time safeguards, to its widest audience so far.
