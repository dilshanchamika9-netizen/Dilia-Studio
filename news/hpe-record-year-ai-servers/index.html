HPE sales jump 50% on AI servers and it raises next year's outlook

Server revenue nearly doubled in the quarter. HPE now expects 2027 revenue of $59.4 billion to $63.1 billion.

Dilia Pulse deskMonday 23 November 20262 min read

Hewlett Packard Enterprise reported revenue of $14.5 billion for its fiscal fourth quarter, which ended on 31 October, an increase of 50.3 per cent from a year earlier.

The surge came from its Cloud and AI segment, where revenue rose 68.6 per cent to $11.3 billion. Within that, server sales almost doubled, up 94.7 per cent to $8.8 billion, and storage grew 33.8 per cent. Networking, strengthened by the purchase of Juniper Networks, brought in $3.1 billion, up 12.3 per cent.

Profit

Earnings per diluted share were $1.26 on a GAAP basis and $1.43 on HPE's adjusted basis. For the full fiscal year, revenue reached $46.7 billion, up 36.3 per cent from $34.3 billion.

Outlook

HPE raised its guidance for fiscal 2027. It now expects revenue to grow between 27 and 35 per cent, to between $59.4 billion and $63.1 billion, with adjusted earnings per share of $5.05 to $5.35 and free cash flow of at least $5.5 billion. For the first quarter alone it forecasts revenue of $14.0 billion to $14.6 billion.

Chief executive Antonio Neri described fiscal 2026 as a defining year that turned HPE into a networking leader with a larger role in AI infrastructure. Despite the numbers, the shares fell in early trading on 24 November, according to market reports.
