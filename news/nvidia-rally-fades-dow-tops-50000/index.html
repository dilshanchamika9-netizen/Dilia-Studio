Nvidia gives back part of its surge as the Nasdaq falls and the Dow closes above 50,000

Chip shares sold off a day after Jensen Huang's return lifted the market. Investors also braced for possible new US tariffs.

Dilia Pulse deskTuesday 24 November 20262 min read

US stocks split on Tuesday as the technology rally that followed Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia faded after one session, according to Reuters. The Nasdaq and the S&P 500 closed lower, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose.

The numbers

The Dow gained 423.39 points, or 0.85 per cent, to close at 50,018.70. The S&P 500 lost 36.08 points, or 0.50 per cent, to 7,201.09, and the Nasdaq Composite lost 356.02 points, or 1.48 per cent. Bloomberg reported that the Nasdaq 100 fell about 2 per cent at the close, after rising almost 7 per cent on Monday.

Why chips fell

Nvidia shares slipped about 5.9 per cent, giving back part of Monday's 31 per cent jump. Reuters said investors were weighing political and governance risks around Huang's reinstatement, and were braced for possible new US tariffs. Bloomberg described renewed worries about how long the AI trade can last. A market report from BigGo Finance put the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index down 7.88 per cent and Micron down about 11 per cent.

Money moves elsewhere

BigGo reported that money rotated into sectors outside technology. The Dow closing above 50,000 is a round-number milestone, not a forecast of what comes next.
