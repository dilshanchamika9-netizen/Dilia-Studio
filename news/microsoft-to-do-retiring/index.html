Microsoft will retire its To Do app on 30 June 2027

Work and school users are being moved to Planner. People with personal accounts are told to choose another app, and tasks will not move over automatically.

Dilia Pulse deskWednesday 18 November 20262 min read

Microsoft is retiring To Do, the free task app it built after buying Wunderlist in 2015. The apps on Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android and the web will stop working on 30 June 2027. The underlying service and its sync support will be switched off later, on separate dates.

The news appeared in a Microsoft Planner blog post on 18 November about bringing personal tasks, team plans and project plans into one system.

If you use To Do for work or school

Microsoft is pointing these users to Planner. A redesigned, list-first My tasks page begins rolling out in December 2026. Existing To Do tasks will be visible in Planner until the end of June, but lists and tasks will not be carried across automatically.

If you use a personal account

Planner is not available for personal Microsoft accounts, including Microsoft 365 Personal and Family. Microsoft's advice is to pick another task app. It says it will provide an export tool before the retirement date, but has not yet published details, and an export may not import cleanly elsewhere.

What to do now

Nothing is deleted yet. Microsoft says the app retirement does not schedule user data for deletion. A sensible step is to note which lists you rely on and wait for the export tool before moving.
