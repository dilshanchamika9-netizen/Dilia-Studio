Best Buy posts its strongest sales growth in five years and raises its forecast

Sales at stores open at least a year rose 6.1%. Online sales in the US grew 10.8%, and the company lifted its full-year profit outlook.

Dilia Pulse deskTuesday 24 November 20262 min read

Best Buy reported revenue of $10.24 billion for the quarter that ended on 31 October, 5.8 per cent more than a year earlier. Comparable sales, which count only stores and websites open for at least a year, rose 6.1 per cent. Chief executive Jason Bonfig said that was ahead of expectations and the company's highest growth in five years.

What sold

In the US, comparable sales rose 6.2 per cent. Computing and mobile phones were up 7.1 per cent, services up 8.7 per cent and entertainment up 27.5 per cent. Appliances rose 2.9 per cent and consumer electronics 1.3 per cent. US online comparable sales grew 10.8 per cent, and online made up 33.2 per cent of US revenue.

Profit

Diluted earnings were $1.66 a share, up 152 per cent, and adjusted earnings were $1.62, up 16 per cent. The gross profit rate rose to 23.9 per cent from 23.2 per cent. In the US that was helped by $41 million of IEEPA tariff refunds, and by growth in its marketplace and advertising businesses.

Outlook

Best Buy now expects full-year comparable sales growth of 3.4 to 4.0 per cent, up from 1.9 to 3.0 per cent, and revenue of $43.0 billion to $43.3 billion. It raised its adjusted earnings forecast to $6.90 to $7.05 a share from $6.70 to $6.90. For the fourth quarter it expects comparable sales growth of 2 to 4 per cent. The quarterly dividend is $0.96 a share, payable on 5 January 2027.
