How to play

  • Tap a piece, then a marked square. Pieces move one step diagonally forward.
  • You must jump an enemy piece when you can, and keep jumping if more jumps follow.
  • Reach the far row to become a king, which moves and jumps both ways.
  • Win by taking all enemy pieces or leaving them with no move. Keys: U undo, F flip.

About Checkers

This is American checkers (English draughts) on an 8 by 8 board: men move one step forward, kings move one step in any direction, and capturing is compulsory. A man that reaches the last row is crowned and its turn ends. Forty moves each without a capture or a man moving is a draw.

Beginner and Casual make mistakes. Club thinks several jumps ahead. Expert searches deepest and is hard to beat.

The computer runs inside your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. Undo is unlimited, and your game stays in this browser.
