Flight time and jet lag

When you land in local time, how the clocks move, your layover, and a simple jet-lag plan.

Working…
Trip plannerWorld clock

How it works

Type the departure time shown on your ticket (local time where you leave) and the flight length. Arrival is worked out with the time zone rules built into your browser, including daylight saving on both ends. The connection time is the local time at the airport where you change planes; if it is earlier than your landing, the next day is used.

The jet-lag tips are general guidance used by travel-health services, not medical advice.
