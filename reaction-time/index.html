About the reaction time test

Wait for the box to turn green, then tap. The time counts from the moment the green is drawn to your tap. It includes the delay of your screen, device and browser, so use it to compare yourself on the same device, not against other people's numbers on other devices.

Typical results are in the range of 200 to 300 milliseconds. This is a fun test, not a medical one. Your times stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
