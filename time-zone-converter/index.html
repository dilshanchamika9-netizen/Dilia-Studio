How it works

Times use the time zone rules built into your browser, including daylight saving. When clocks move forward, some times do not exist; when they move back, a time can happen twice. Both cases are shown. Work hours are taken as 09:00 to 18:00 local time.

Everything is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
