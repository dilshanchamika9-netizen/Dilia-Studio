How to Convert an Image to PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF and More

A website refuses your WebP, a form wants a JPG, or you need a PNG with a clear background. Convert image changes the type in your browser, and the file name stays the same except for the ending.

Open Convert image

Steps

  1. Open Convert image and drop your pictures in.
  2. Choose the type to save as, for example JPG or PNG.
  3. For JPG, WebP and AVIF, set the quality. 90 is a good default.
  4. Press convert and download. The new files keep their original names with the new ending.

Transparent areas turn white in JPG

JPG cannot hold transparency, so clear areas become white. If you need to keep a clear background, save as PNG or WebP.

Which type should I pick?

JPG suits photos and works everywhere. PNG suits logos, screenshots and anything with sharp edges or transparency. WebP and AVIF are usually smaller at the same look, but not every older program opens them.

Variant pages

The site also has direct pages such as PNG to JPG, WebP to JPG and Image to ICO. They are the same tool with the output already chosen.

Questions

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser tab.

Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes. Use HEIC to JPG, or drop the HEIC files here.

Will the quality change?

Converting to JPG, WebP or AVIF applies the quality you set. PNG and BMP keep every pixel.
