How to Extract Emails, Links and Numbers from a Text

Instead of hunting through a long text, Extract from text lists every email, link, number or hashtag in it. Nothing leaves your browser.

Open Extract from text

Steps

  1. Open Extract from text and paste your text.
  2. Choose what to find.
  3. Tick "Only once each" to remove repeats, and sort if you like.
  4. Copy the list or download it.

Phone numbers

Phone detection is a guess based on digit patterns. Check the list before you use it.

Links

Trailing full stops and commas are removed from links.

Questions

Is my text uploaded?

No.

Does it validate emails?

It finds text shaped like an email. It does not check that the address exists.
