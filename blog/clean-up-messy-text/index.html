How to Remove Extra Spaces, Line Breaks and Odd Characters

Text copied from a PDF or email often arrives with broken lines, double spaces and hidden characters. Text cleaner fixes these in your browser.

Open Text cleaner

Steps

  1. Open Text cleaner and paste your text.
  2. Tick the clean-ups you want, for example extra spaces or line breaks.
  3. Check the result on the right.
  4. Copy it or download it as a TXT file.

Text copied from a PDF

Tick "Remove line breaks" and "Keep paragraphs apart when joining" to turn broken lines into flowing paragraphs.

Accents and special characters

Removing accents turns é into e. Removing special characters also removes punctuation, so use it only when you want plain words.

Hidden characters

Zero-width spaces and soft hyphens are invisible but break searches. The invisible characters option removes them.

Questions

Is my text uploaded?

No.

Does it change my emoji?

Only if you tick "Remove emoji".
