How to Make a Meme From Your Own Picture

A meme is a picture with bold text on the top and bottom. Meme generator draws that text for you and shows a live preview while you type, so you can get the wording right before you download.

Open Meme generator

Steps

  1. Open Meme generator and drop your picture in.
  2. Type the top text and the bottom text. The preview above updates as you type.
  3. Change the text size or colour if needed. White with a black outline is the classic look.
  4. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP, press the button and download.

Long lines shrink to fit

If your text is long, it wraps to more lines and the letters get smaller so they stay inside the top or bottom part of the picture.

Use your own pictures

Pictures from your phone or camera are best. Using a picture of a person or a brand you do not own can cause problems when you share it, so check the rights first.

One picture at a time

This tool makes one meme per download. For many pictures with one stamp, use Watermark image.

Questions

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Drawing happens in your browser tab.

Why are the letters capitals?

The classic meme style uses capitals, so the text is shown in capitals.

Can I move the text?

Not yet. Text stays at the top and bottom.
