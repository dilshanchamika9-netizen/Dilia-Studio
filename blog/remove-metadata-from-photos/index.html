How to Remove Location and Camera Data From Photos

A photo can carry the camera model, the date and time, and sometimes the exact place where it was taken. Before you post or send a picture it is worth checking. Remove metadata shows what is inside each photo and removes it, in your browser.

Open Remove metadata

Steps

  1. Open Remove metadata and drop your JPG or PNG files in.
  2. Each card shows what the photo contains, such as camera, date and GPS.
  3. Press the button. The details are removed without re-compressing the picture, so quality does not change.
  4. Download the clean copies.

Social sites may strip data, but do not count on it

Some platforms remove location from public pictures and others do not. Sending a photo by email or chat usually keeps everything. Cleaning it yourself first is the sure way.

Which files are supported?

JPG and PNG. For other types, convert to JPG or PNG first.

Does anything else identify the picture?

The picture itself may show a street sign, a house number or a face. Use Blur image areas for those.

Questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Reading and cleaning happen in your browser.

Will the picture look different?

No. Only the hidden notes are removed. The pixels are untouched.

Does it remove the date from the file name?

No. Rename the file yourself if the name shows a date.
