How to Count Words and Characters in Any Text

Essays, captions and job applications often have a limit. Word counter shows words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and reading time as you type. Your text stays in your browser.

Open Word counter

Steps

  1. Open Word counter and paste or type your text. You can also press "Open TXT, DOCX or PDF".
  2. Read the counts on the right. They update as you type.
  3. Pick a limit from the list, such as X or Instagram, to see how many characters are left.
  4. Check "Most used words" to spot words you repeat too often.

Why counts differ between programs

Programs disagree on hyphens, numbers and symbols. Here a word is a run of letters or numbers, and a hyphen or apostrophe inside it does not split it. Expect small differences.

Scanned PDFs have no text

A PDF made from photos has no text to count. Use OCR first, then count the result.

Readability is for English only

The Flesch score and grade use English syllable rules. They are estimates and should not be used for other languages.

Questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser.

Does it count Chinese or Japanese?

Only as characters. Word counts need spaces between words.

How is reading time worked out?

About 200 words a minute for reading and 130 for speaking.
