How to Sign a PDF Without Printing It

Printing a document, signing it by hand and scanning it again wastes time. You can place your signature on the PDF directly. Sign PDF lets you draw it with a finger or mouse, type it, or use a photo of your real signature.

Open Sign PDF

Steps

  1. Open Sign PDF and drop the document in.
  2. Create your signature: draw it, type your name and pick a style, or upload an image.
  3. Click the page where it should go, then drag and resize it to fit the line.
  4. Save and download the signed copy. The original file is not changed.

Tips for a clean signature

If you upload a photo of your signature, sign on plain white paper in dark ink and crop the picture close. A signature on a light background looks neater on the page.

Adding the date and your name

Use the Add text tool to type the date next to the signature, and fill in form fields if the document has them. Everything is saved into one file.

What kind of signature is this?

This places an image of your signature on the page, like signing on paper. It is not a certificate-based digital signature that proves who signed and detects later changes. Most everyday documents only need the first kind, but some contracts, tenders and government forms require a certified signature. Check what the other side asks for before you send.

Keep your signature private

Your signature image stays in your browser and is not uploaded. Download the signed copy and close the tab when you are done on a shared computer.

Questions

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Draw the signature with your finger on the screen.

Can I sign several pages?

Yes. Place the signature on each page that needs it, or use Duplicate to copy it.

Can I lock the document after signing?

You can protect it with a password, which stops people opening it without the password.
