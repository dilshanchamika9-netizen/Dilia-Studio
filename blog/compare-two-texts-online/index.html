How to Compare Two Texts and See What Changed

When two versions of a contract, article or list look alike, Compare two texts shows exactly what was added or removed. The comparison runs in your browser.

Open Compare two texts

Steps

  1. Open Compare two texts.
  2. Paste the original on the left and the changed text on the right.
  3. Choose to compare by line, word or character.
  4. Tick "Ignore capital letters" or "Ignore extra spaces" if those changes do not matter.

Which level to use

By line suits lists and code. By word suits paragraphs. By character suits short strings such as codes.

Very large texts

Two very different long texts may be too big for word or character mode. Use By line, or compare smaller parts.
