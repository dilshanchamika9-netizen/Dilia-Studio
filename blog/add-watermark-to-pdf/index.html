How to Add a Watermark to a PDF

A watermark tells people a document is a draft, confidential or a copy. It also discourages casual re-use. Watermark PDF adds text or a logo to every page, and the preview shows exactly where it will land.

Open Watermark PDF

Steps

  1. Open Watermark and drop your PDF in.
  2. Choose Text or Image. Type your words or pick your logo.
  3. Adjust the strength, colour, angle and layout. The preview updates on every page.
  4. Save and download the watermarked PDF.

Keep it readable

A light, semi-transparent watermark lets people still read the page. A heavy one makes the document hard to use and can annoy the person you send it to.

Confidential documents

A watermark is a label, not a lock. For documents that must not be opened by the wrong people, also protect the file with a password.

Using your logo

Use a PNG with a transparent background so the logo does not show a white box on the page.

Questions

Can the watermark be removed?

It becomes part of the page. Someone with the right tools may be able to remove it, so do not treat it as a security measure.

Can I watermark only some pages?

The watermark is applied to all pages. Extract the pages you want first if you need only part.

Is it free and private?

Yes. It runs in your browser.
