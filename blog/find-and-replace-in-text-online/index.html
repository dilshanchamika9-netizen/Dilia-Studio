How to Find and Replace Words in a Text

Replace a name, a typo or a pattern across a whole text at once. Your text is only changed in the result box until you choose to keep it.

Open Find and replace

Steps

  1. Open Find and replace and paste your text.
  2. Type what to find and what to put instead.
  3. Tick Match case or Whole words if needed.
  4. Copy the result, or press "Use result as input" to keep working.

Whole words

Replacing "cat" without Whole words also changes "category". Tick the option to avoid it.

Patterns

Patterns (regular expressions) are for advanced use. $1 in the replacement stands for the first bracketed group. An invalid pattern shows an error and changes nothing.
