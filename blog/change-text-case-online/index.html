How to Change Text to Uppercase, Lowercase or Title Case

Text typed with caps lock on, or a headline in the wrong style, takes seconds to fix with Case converter. Nothing leaves your browser.

Open Case converter

Steps

  1. Open Case converter and paste your text.
  2. Press the case you want. The result appears on the right.
  3. Press Copy result, or Download TXT.
  4. Press "Use result as input" to chain another change.

Title Case rules

Small words such as "of", "the" and "and" stay lowercase unless they start or end the title. Style guides differ, so check names and acronyms by eye.

Sentence case

Everything becomes lowercase, then the first letter of each sentence and the word "I" are capitalised. Names in the middle of a sentence are lowercased, so fix them by hand.

camelCase and snake_case

These work line by line, which suits lists of variable or column names.

Questions

Is my text uploaded?

No.

Can I undo?

Your original text stays in the left box. Only "Use result as input" replaces it.
