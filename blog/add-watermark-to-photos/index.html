How to Add a Watermark to Photos

A watermark shows who made a picture and makes casual copying less tempting. Watermark image adds text or your logo to many pictures at once, in your browser.

Open Watermark image

Steps

  1. Open Watermark image and drop your pictures in.
  2. Choose Text and type your words, or choose Logo image and pick your logo file.
  3. Set the position, size and opacity. Add an angle if you want it slanted.
  4. Tick "Repeat across the image" to cover the whole picture, then press the button and download.

A watermark does not make copying impossible

Anyone can crop or paint over a corner watermark. Repeating it across the picture is harder to remove, but still not a lock. Treat it as a label, not protection.

Keep it readable but quiet

Around 40 percent opacity works on most photos. Use a light colour on dark pictures and a dark colour on light ones.

Use a logo with a clear background

A PNG logo with transparency sits on the picture cleanly. A logo with a white box shows the box.

Questions

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Everything is done in your browser.

Is the original changed?

No. You download new copies.

Can I watermark many pictures with one setting?

Yes. The same watermark is applied to every picture you add.
