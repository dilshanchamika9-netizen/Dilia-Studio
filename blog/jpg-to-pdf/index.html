How to Convert JPG and PNG Photos to a PDF

Forms, receipts and ID copies often must be sent as one PDF, but you only have photos on your phone. Images to PDF puts them together, in the order you want, on pages of the size you choose.

Open Images to PDF

Steps

  1. Open Images to PDF and drop your photos on the page. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF and AVIF work.
  2. Drag the images into the right order and turn any that are sideways.
  3. Pick a page size: same as the image, A4, or US Letter.
  4. Name the file and download your PDF.

Which page size?

Choose A4 or US Letter when the PDF will be printed or filed with others. Choose same as image when you want each page to match its photo with no white borders.

Taking better photos of documents

Lay the page flat in good light, avoid shadows from your hand, and keep the camera straight above the sheet. If a scan is slightly crooked, the Straighten tool can fix it after you create the PDF.

Making the file smaller

Phone photos are large. Several of them can make a PDF too big to email. Run the result through Compress PDF before you send it.

Scanning with your camera

There is also a Scan to PDF tool which uses your phone camera to capture pages one after another.

Questions

Can I mix JPG and PNG in one PDF?

Yes. Mix formats freely.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They stay on your device while the PDF is built.

Will the photos lose quality?

They are placed in the PDF as they are, so quality is kept.
