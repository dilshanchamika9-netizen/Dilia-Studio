How to Resize an Image for Instagram, YouTube and the Web

Every platform wants a different size, and a picture that is too large is slow to load or gets cropped in the wrong place. Resize image lets you type exact pixels, scale by percent, or use a preset. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Open Resize image

Steps

  1. Open Resize image and drop your pictures in.
  2. Pick a preset such as Instagram post 1080 × 1080, or choose Pixels and type a width or height, or choose Percent.
  3. Keep "Keep proportions" ticked so the picture is not stretched. Leave "Never enlarge" ticked so small pictures are not blown up and blurred.
  4. Press resize and download. Many images can be done at once and saved as a ZIP.

Presets are exact sizes, not smart crops

A preset sets the size. If your picture has a different shape, it is fitted and not cut. If you want the picture trimmed to a shape first, use Crop image, then resize.

Why the picture looks soft after enlarging

Making a small picture bigger cannot add detail that was never there. That is why "Never enlarge" is on by default. Turn it off only when you must reach an exact size.

Resize and file size are not the same thing

Fewer pixels usually means a smaller file, but the quality setting matters too. To reach a size limit in KB, resize first, then use Compress image with a target size.

Questions

Which size does Instagram use?

Square posts commonly use 1080 × 1080 and stories use 1080 × 1920. The presets include both.

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Resizing happens inside your browser tab.

Can I resize many pictures at once?

Yes. The same settings are applied to every picture you add.
