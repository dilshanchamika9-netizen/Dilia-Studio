How to Crop an Image to a Ratio or Circle

Cropping removes what you do not need and fixes the shape for a profile photo, a banner or a thumbnail. Crop image offers common ratios and a circle, and it runs in your browser.

Open Crop image

Steps

  1. Open Crop image and drop your picture in.
  2. Choose a shape: Free, Square, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16, 4:5 or Circle.
  3. Press the pencil on the picture and drag the box to the part you want to keep. Pictures you leave alone are cropped from the centre.
  4. Press crop and download.

Circle crops are saved as PNG

A circle needs transparent corners, and only PNG can hold them. The tool saves a circle crop as PNG for that reason. If you then save it as JPG somewhere else, the corners turn white.

Crop many pictures to the same shape

Pick one shape and add all pictures. Pictures you did not set a box for are cropped from the centre, which works well for faces and products placed in the middle.

Crop first, then resize

If you need an exact size, crop to the right shape here and then use Resize image. Doing it in that order keeps the picture sharp.

Questions

Is the original changed?

No. You download a new file. Your original stays on your device untouched.

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Cropping happens in your browser.

Can I rotate while cropping?

Use Rotate and flip first, then crop.
