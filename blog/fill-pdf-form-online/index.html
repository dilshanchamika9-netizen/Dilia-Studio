How to Fill In a PDF Form Online

Many applications and forms still arrive as PDFs. You do not need to print them and write by hand. If the PDF has fillable fields, Fill forms lets you type, tick and choose, then save a filled copy.

Open Fill PDF form

Steps

  1. Open Fill forms and drop the form in. Fields are found and shown on the page.
  2. Click a field and type. Tick boxes, choose radio buttons and pick from lists where they appear.
  3. If something does not fit a field, you can also add free text anywhere on the page.
  4. Save and download the filled form.

Forms without fillable fields

Some PDFs look like forms but are only flat pages. There are no fields to click. Use the Text tool to place text on the lines, and add your signature with Sign PDF.

Making your own form

If you need people to fill in a document you made, Create form adds text fields, tick boxes and lists to any PDF.

Before you send

Read through the page once. If the form will be printed or archived, keep a copy of the filled file for your records.

Questions

Why can I not click anything?

The PDF may have no fillable fields. Use the Text tool instead.

Is my information uploaded?

No. The form is filled in your browser.

Can I sign it too?

Yes. Open the filled copy in Sign PDF.
