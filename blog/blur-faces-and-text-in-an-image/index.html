How to Blur Faces, Plates and Text in a Picture

Before you share a screenshot or photo you may need to hide a face, a number plate or an address. Blur image areas lets you draw boxes over the parts to hide, in your browser.

Open Blur image areas

Steps

  1. Open Blur image areas and drop your picture in.
  2. Choose how to cover: Blur, Pixels or Black box.
  3. Press the pencil on the picture and drag over each area to hide.
  4. Press the button and download. Check the result before you share it.

Black box is the safest

Blur and pixel effects hide details, but a light blur over small text can sometimes still be guessed. For passwords, card numbers and ID numbers, use a black box.

The saved file really changes

The hidden pixels are replaced in the new picture. They are not just covered by a layer that someone could remove.

Remove the hidden data too

Photos can carry the place and date. After hiding the faces, use Remove metadata so the file does not give the location away.

Questions

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I undo a box?

Draw a new set of boxes from the original picture. Your original file is never changed.

Does it find faces automatically?

No. You choose the areas yourself.
