How to Delete Pages From a PDF

A blank page at the end, a cover sheet you do not need, or pages you do not want to share. Deleting pages makes the file smaller and keeps private pages private. Delete pages lets you click the pages to remove or type their numbers.

Open Delete PDF pages

Steps

  1. Open Delete pages and drop your PDF in.
  2. Click each page you want to remove, or type numbers and ranges such as 2, 5-7.
  3. Check the count of pages that will remain.
  4. Download the new PDF. Your original file is untouched.

Gone means gone

The new PDF contains only the pages you kept. The deleted pages are not hidden inside the file, so the person you send it to cannot recover them from it.

Keep only a few pages instead

If you want just a few pages, Extract pages is quicker: pick the pages to keep rather than the ones to remove.

Check before you send

Open the new file and scroll through it once. Page numbers printed on the pages do not change, so a gap in the numbering is normal.

Questions

Can I undo a deletion?

Your original file is not changed. If you removed the wrong page, start again from the original.

Will the file get smaller?

Usually, because the removed pages are not included.

Is it private?

Yes. The file stays in your browser.
