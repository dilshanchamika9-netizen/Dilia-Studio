How to Generate Lorem Ipsum Placeholder Text

Designers use placeholder text to test layouts before real content exists. Lorem ipsum generator makes it in your browser.

Open Lorem ipsum generator

Steps

  1. Open Lorem ipsum generator.
  2. Choose paragraphs, sentences or words and how many.
  3. Keep "Start with Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet" ticked if you want the classic opening.
  4. Copy the result or download it.

New text each time

Press "Make new text" for a different version with the same settings.

Limits

Up to 100 paragraphs, 500 sentences or 5000 words at once.
