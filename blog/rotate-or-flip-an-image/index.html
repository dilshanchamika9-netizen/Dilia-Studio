How to Rotate or Flip an Image (Free, No Upload)

A photo opens sideways, or text in a picture is mirrored. Rotate and flip fixes that in a few seconds and saves a new copy, without sending your picture anywhere.

Open Rotate and flip

Steps

  1. Open Rotate and flip and drop your pictures in.
  2. Choose Right 90°, Left 90° or Upside down 180°.
  3. Tick "Left to right" or "Top to bottom" if you want a mirror image.
  4. Press the button and download.

The same turn is applied to every picture

If you add several pictures, all of them get the same turn. Do the sideways ones in one batch and the others in another.

Why a photo looks fine on a phone but sideways elsewhere

Phones often store a small note saying "show this turned" instead of turning the pixels. Some programs ignore that note. Saving a rotated copy writes the turn into the picture itself.

Questions

Does rotating lower the quality?

The picture is saved again, so set the quality high if you need the best result. The default is high.

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I turn by a custom angle?

Not in this tool. It turns in steps of 90 degrees and mirrors.
