PDF how-to guides

Short, honest guides for everyday PDF jobs. Each one tells you what the tool does and what it cannot do.

Edit text keeps the original font where it can.How to Sign a PDF Without Printing ItDraw, type or upload your signature and place it on a PDF. No printing, no scanning, no account.How to Split a PDF Into Separate Pages or FilesCut one PDF into several. Split every page, every few pages or by ranges you type. Free and private.How to Protect a PDF With a PasswordLock a PDF with a password using AES-256 encryption in your browser.
