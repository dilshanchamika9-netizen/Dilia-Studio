How to Open iPhone HEIC Photos as JPG (Free, No Upload)

iPhones save photos as HEIC to save space. Many Windows programs, websites and forms cannot open them. HEIC to JPG turns them into ordinary JPG files in your browser, so your private photos stay on your device.

Open HEIC to JPG

Steps

  1. Open HEIC to JPG and drop your .heic files on the page, or choose them from your device.
  2. Leave the quality at the default, or raise it for the best result.
  3. Press convert. Each photo shows its new size.
  4. Download one file, or all of them as a ZIP.

Large batches can be slow on a phone

Decoding HEIC is heavy work. If a phone struggles, convert a few photos at a time.

Dates and location

Photos usually carry the date and sometimes the place they were taken. If you plan to share them, run the results through Remove metadata first.

If a file will not open

A few HEIC files from other apps use settings this tool cannot read. If one fails, the card says so and the others continue.

Questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs inside your browser.

Will the file name stay?

Yes. IMG_1234.heic becomes IMG_1234.jpg.

Can I save as PNG instead?

Yes. Use the HEIC to PNG page or Convert image.
