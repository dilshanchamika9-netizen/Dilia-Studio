How to Rotate PDF Pages and Save Them That Way

A scan comes out sideways, or one page of a report is landscape and shows upside down. Turning the view in your PDF reader only lasts until you close it. Rotate PDF changes the pages themselves, so every reader shows them upright.

Open Rotate PDF

Steps

  1. Open Rotate PDF and drop your file in.
  2. Hover a page and use its arrows to turn it left or right. Use the buttons at the top to turn the whole file.
  3. Check each page in the preview.
  4. Save and download the new PDF.

Only fix the pages that need it

Turn single pages when only some are wrong. Each page keeps its own turn, so a landscape table can stay sideways while the rest are upright.

Scans that are slightly crooked

Rotation works in quarter turns. If a scan is a few degrees off, use Straighten scanned pages, which measures the tilt and corrects it.

After rotating

If you still need to merge, split or sign the file, do it on the rotated copy so every step sees the pages the right way up.

Questions

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. The page is turned, not re-drawn.

Is the file uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser.

Can I rotate by 45 degrees?

No. Rotation is in quarter turns. Use Straighten for small tilts in scans.
