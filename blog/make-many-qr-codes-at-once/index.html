How to Make Many QR Codes at Once

Product labels, table numbers, event tickets: sometimes you need dozens of QR codes that look the same but point to different places. Bulk QR codes turns a list into a ZIP of files in one go, in your browser.

Open Bulk QR codes

Steps

  1. Open Bulk QR codes and choose the kind of entries, for example links.
  2. Paste your list with one entry on each line.
  3. Set the colours and shape once. They apply to every code. Choose PNG or SVG for the files.
  4. Press the button and download the ZIP.

Check the first and last code

Scan the first and the last code from the ZIP with a phone before printing the whole set. It catches a stray space or a missing line.

Keep entries similar in length

Very long entries make a denser code. Short links keep every code easy to scan at the same printed size.

Name your files

By default files are numbered qr-001, qr-002 and so on, in the order of your list. Tick the naming option and start each line with a name and a comma, such as table-5, https://example.com/5, to name the files yourself.

Questions

How many codes can I make?

Up to 2000 lines at a time. Several hundred is fine on a computer; use smaller batches on a phone.

Are my links uploaded?

No. The codes and the ZIP are built in your browser.

Do all codes look the same?

Yes. The colours and shape you choose are applied to every code, only the content changes.
