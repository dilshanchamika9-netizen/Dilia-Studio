How the planner works

It finds the work days to take as leave so that, together with weekends and public holidays, they make the longest breaks. "One long break" puts your leave into the biggest single trip; "many short breaks" spreads it into long weekends. Pick your own weekend days: many countries in the Gulf use Friday and Saturday, and some use only Friday.

Holidays are national only, 2024 to 2035, from the open-source Vacanza holidays library (MIT licence). Regional, school and private-sector holidays can differ. A holiday that falls on a weekend is not moved here; some countries give a day off instead. Weekend defaults by country are a starting point.
