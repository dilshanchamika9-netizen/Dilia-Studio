How it works

Month and year names come from the calendar tables built into your browser, so they match what the browser knows for each calendar. The Julian date, day numbers and ISO week are worked out on this page.

Islamic and Chinese months depend on the moon. Umm al-Qura and the tabular calendar can differ by a day, and a country that sights the moon may differ too. Treat the Hijri date as a guide for planning and check with a local authority for religious observance. The Hebrew day starts at sunset the evening before.
