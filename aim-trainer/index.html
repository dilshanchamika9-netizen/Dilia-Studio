How to play

  • Press Start.
  • Tap or click each target as soon as it appears. Only one shows at a time.
  • Targets shrink as you hit them. Missing a target counts against your accuracy.
  • After 30 seconds you see hits, accuracy and average time per target.

About Aim trainer

Aim trainers measure how fast and precisely you can point. Your result is hits in 30 seconds, plus accuracy and average reaction time per target. Targets shrink a little with every hit.

Your scores stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
