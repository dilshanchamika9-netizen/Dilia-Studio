Build a list for your trip, tick items off and print it. It also includes a before-you-leave checklist.

How it works

Pick the trip length and type and the page adds a sensible starting list. The clothes counts assume you can wash once a week, so they stop growing after about a week. Add or remove anything. Lists are saved in this browser only, and the Trip booklet can print one. Check the rules of your airline and destination for what you can carry.
