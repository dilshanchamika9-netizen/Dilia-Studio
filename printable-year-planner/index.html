Printable year planner

A one-page wall calendar with your country's public holidays and your own dates. Print it or save it as a PDF.

    Holiday plannerTeam leave planner

    How it works

    Pick a country and year and the planner shades weekends and public holidays. Add your own dates and they are marked in the calendar and listed below it. In the print window choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to get a file, and keep margins on Default or None. Days that follow the moon, such as Eid, are starred because the date can shift by a day.

    Your settings and dates are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
