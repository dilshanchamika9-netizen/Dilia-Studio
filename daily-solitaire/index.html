How to play

  • Build four home piles from Ace to King, one per suit.
  • In the columns, put a card on one rank higher of the other colour. Only a King goes on an empty column.
  • Tap the deck to turn cards. Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it.
  • Keys: D turns a card, U or Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Today's deal is the same for everyone and can be won.

About daily solitaire

One new Klondike deal every day at midnight where you are, the same for everyone. Every daily deal has been checked by a solver and can be won. Draw one card at a time, with as many passes through the deck as you like.

Your game and times stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
