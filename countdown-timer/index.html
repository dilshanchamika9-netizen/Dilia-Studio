Countdown timer

Run several timers at once. They keep counting if you close the tab or reload.

Stopwatch

How it works

Each timer stores the moment it will end, so it stays correct when the tab sleeps, the phone locks or the page reloads. When a timer ends you hear a short alarm (made in your browser, no sound file) and the phone vibrates if it can. Browsers only play sound after you have tapped something on the page, so start the timer yourself rather than relying on an old tab. The soonest timer also shows in the tab title.

Your timers are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
