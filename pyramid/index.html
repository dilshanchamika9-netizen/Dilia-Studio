How to play

  • Tap two free cards that add up to 13 to take them away. A free King goes on its own.
  • Ace is 1, Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13.
  • A card is free when no card lies on top of it. Use the card on the waste pile as a partner too.
  • Tap the deck for the next card, as many times round as you like. Clear all 28 pyramid cards to win.

About Pyramid

Clear the pyramid by pairing free cards that add up to 13, or by removing Kings alone. The deck turns one card at a time and you can go through it as many times as you like. With Winnable deals on, every deal has been checked by a solver before you see it. Undo as much as you like.
