How to play

  • Pick a speed and a text, then press Start.
  • Keep your eyes on the highlighted letter. Words appear one at a time. Tap the words to pause.
  • Answer three questions to see if you understood what you read.
  • You can paste your own text instead. No questions are asked for your own text.

About Speed reading

This is rapid serial visual presentation: one word at a time in the same spot, so your eyes do not have to move. Reading speed with good understanding for most adults is around 200 to 300 words per minute. Very high speeds mostly lose detail, so always check your understanding. Texts are original and written for this page.

Your scores stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
