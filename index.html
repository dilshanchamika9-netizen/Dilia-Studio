      Questions

      Is Dilia free?

      Yes. Every tool works without an account, a limit or a watermark.

      Where do my files go?

      Nowhere. They are opened and changed by your browser on your own device. They are not sent to a server.

      Does it work on a phone?

      Yes, in any modern browser. Scan to PDF can use your phone camera.

      Which files can I drop on this page?

      PDFs and pictures (JPG, PNG, TIFF and more). Dilia shows the tools that fit, then opens the one you pick with your file ready.

      What is coming next?

      More Life helpers (holiday and trip planners), more daily games and more guides. They will run on your device too.
