How to play

  • Tap a square to open it. A number says how many mines touch it.
  • Long-press, right-click or use Flag mode to mark a mine.
  • Tap a number when all its mines are flagged to open the rest around it.
  • Keyboard: arrows, Enter to open, F to flag.
  • Your first tap is always safe. With No guessing on, every board can be solved by logic alone.

About minesweeper

Classic minesweeper with three levels (9 × 9, 16 × 16 and 30 × 16) and your own custom size. On a phone the expert board turns tall so the squares stay big enough to tap. No guessing mode makes boards that can always be solved by logic.
