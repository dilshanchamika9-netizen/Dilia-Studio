About this card

Each fact is shown only when two open sources agree. When a country has just changed something (Bulgaria moved to the euro in 2026, for example), the sources can disagree for a while, and the card says so instead of guessing.

Emergency numbers, plug types and voltage are not shown yet: they are being checked against official sources first. Tipping customs are not shown because there is no reliable source.
