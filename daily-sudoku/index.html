How to play

  • Fill every row, column and 3 × 3 box with 1 to 9.
  • Pick a square, then tap a number or type it.
  • Notes: small pencil marks. Press N to switch.
  • Numbers that clash turn pink.

About the daily sudoku

Three new boards arrive every day at midnight where you are: easy, medium and hard. Everyone gets the same boards on the same date, and each board has exactly one answer.

Hints fill one square and are shown in your result. Your board, time and streak are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
