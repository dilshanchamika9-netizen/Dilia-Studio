How to play

  • Roll the dice. A 6 brings a token out of the base and gives you another roll.
  • Move a token the number shown. Tap one of the glowing tokens.
  • Land on an enemy token to send it back to base. Star squares and start squares are safe.
  • Walk all the way round, then into your coloured lane. You need the exact number to get home. First to bring all 4 tokens home wins.

About Ludo

Ludo is a race game for two to four players. Each player has four tokens that start in the base. A 6 brings a token onto the track and gives another roll; three 6s in a row lose the turn. Tokens travel once around the board and then up their own coloured lane. A token needs the exact number to reach home.

Play against computers at four levels, or pass the device between friends. The dice use your browser's secure random generator, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your game stays in this browser.
