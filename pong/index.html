How to play

  • Move the mouse, drag, or use W and S (or the arrow keys) to move your paddle.
  • Tap or press Space to serve.
  • Hit the ball with the paddle edge to send it at a sharper angle. It speeds up every hit.
  • First to 7 wins. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard.
