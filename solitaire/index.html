How to play

  • Build four home piles from Ace to King, one per suit.
  • In the columns, put a card on one rank higher of the other colour. Only a King goes on an empty column.
  • Tap the deck to turn cards. Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it.
  • Keys: D turns a card, U or Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Winnable deals (draw 1): every deal can be won.

About solitaire

Classic Klondike solitaire with draw 1 or draw 3 and unlimited passes through the deck. With Winnable deals on, every draw-1 deal has been checked by a solver before you see it. Undo as much as you like; when all cards are face up, Finish sends them home.
