About the bingo card generator

Classic cards follow the 75-ball rules: column B holds numbers 1 to 15, I 16 to 30, N 31 to 45, G 46 to 60 and O 61 to 75, with a free space in the middle. With your own words, each card takes 24 of your words in a random order.

Cards are made on your device and printed from your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
