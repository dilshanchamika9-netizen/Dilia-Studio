How to play

  • Choose Play White or Play Black, then press Start game (as White you can also just move).
  • Tap a piece, then a green mark. Or drag it.
  • All rules are in: castling, en passant, promotion, check, stalemate and draws.
  • Undo takes back your last move (and the reply). Keys: U undo, F flip board.
  • Your game is saved in this browser.

    About Chess

    Play the full game of chess against a computer opponent at four levels, or pass the device between two players. The computer is an engine written for Dilia that runs inside your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. Beginner and Casual make human-like mistakes. Club plays solid, tactical chess. Expert searches deeper and uses the full thinking time, and on a slow phone it will play weaker than on a computer.

    Draws are applied automatically: stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and positions where neither side can mate. Undo is unlimited. Your game stays in this browser.
