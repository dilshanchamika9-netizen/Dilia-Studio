Working days

How it works

Both dates are counted. Weekend days are never counted, and public holidays that fall on a working day are skipped when you pick a country. Holidays are national only, from the open-source Vacanza holidays library (MIT licence), 2024 to 2035.

Everything is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
