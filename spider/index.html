How to play

  • Build each column down in rank, King to Ace, on any suit.
  • Only a run of one suit moves together.
  • A full King-to-Ace run of one suit leaves the table. Take off all 8 to win.
  • Tap the deck to deal one card on each column (no empty columns allowed). Keys: D deals, U undoes.

About Spider

Spider uses two packs of cards. Choose 1 suit for a relaxed game, 2 suits for a fair challenge, or 4 suits for the full test. Deals are random, and with 4 suits many of them cannot be won, so use undo freely. One-suit deals are almost always winnable.
