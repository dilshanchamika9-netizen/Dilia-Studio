How it works

  • Click the box and type the words. Press the space bar after each word.
  • The timer starts at your first key. Esc starts again.
  • Speed counts only words typed exactly right (5 letters = 1 word). Accuracy counts every letter.
  • Arabic and Hindi need that language on your keyboard.

    About the typing test

    Choose English, Arabic, Spanish, Hindi or Portuguese, and 15, 30 or 60 seconds. The words are common everyday words, picked at random each time. Your speed is shown in words per minute (WPM), where five letters count as one word, and only words typed exactly right count. Accuracy is the share of letters you typed correctly.

    Your best scores and last ten tests stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
