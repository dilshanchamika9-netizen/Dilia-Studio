About these holidays

These are national (country-wide) public holidays from the open-source Vacanza holidays library. Regional, state, school and private-sector holidays can differ, and governments sometimes add or move a day at short notice.

Holidays that follow the moon, such as Eid, are marked "may shift": the official date is announced close to the day. Long weekends count a holiday joined to the weekend into three or more days off in a row, using that country's usual weekend days.
