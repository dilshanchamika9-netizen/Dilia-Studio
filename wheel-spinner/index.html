    About the wheel spinner

    The winner is chosen first by a fair random pick, and then the wheel is turned so it stops on that entry. Every entry has exactly the same chance, whatever the size of its slice looks like.

    Your entries and recent results stay in this browser. The share link holds the entries inside the address itself, so nothing is stored on a server.
