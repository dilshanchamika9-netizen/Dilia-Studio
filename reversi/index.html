How to play

  • Tap a marked square to place a disc. It must trap a line of enemy discs between your new disc and one of yours.
  • Trapped discs flip to your colour.
  • If you have no legal move you pass. The game ends when neither side can move.
  • Most discs wins. Corners cannot be flipped. Keys: U undo, F flip.

About Reversi

Reversi is played on an 8 by 8 board with discs that are black on one side and white on the other. Black moves first. Every move must flip at least one enemy disc; if you cannot, you pass. When neither player can move, the side with more discs wins.

Corners are strong because they can never be flipped. Beginner and Casual play loosely, Club plans around corners and mobility, and Expert searches deepest.

The computer runs inside your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. Undo is unlimited, and your game stays in this browser.
