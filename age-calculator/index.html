How it works

Age is counted in full years, then full months, then days. Someone born on 29 February is counted as having a birthday on 28 February in years that are not leap years. The life-in-weeks grid shows 90 years of 52 weeks; each tenth year is marked in pink.

Everything is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
