How to play

  • Type a five-letter word and press Enter.
  • Green: right letter, right place. Yellow: the letter is in the word, but elsewhere. Grey: not in the word.
  • You have six guesses. A new word arrives every day, the same for everyone.
  • After you finish, practice with as many extra words as you like.

About Daily word

The daily word comes from our own list of common everyday words, so every answer is a word you know. The word changes at midnight on your device. Your streak is kept in this browser. Any five letters are accepted as a guess, so there is no dictionary rejecting you.

Your scores stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
